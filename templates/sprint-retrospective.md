## Suggested agenda (60 minutes)

| Time | Step | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Set the stage | Restate ground rules and review last retro's actions |
| 10 min | Review the data | Look at sprint metrics and the sprint goal |
| 15 min | Gather feedback | Silent writing on the board, then group similar notes |
| 15 min | Discuss and vote | Talk through the top themes, vote on what to fix |
| 10 min | Decide actions | Pick 1-3 actions, assign owners and dates |
| 5 min | Close | Quick check-out and thank the team |

## Facilitator tips

- Rotate the facilitator so the retro does not become one person's meeting.
- Change the format every few sprints to keep it fresh.
- If the same theme returns three times, it needs a bigger fix than a retro action.
- Review actions at the start of every sprint planning, not only the next retro.
- Celebrate wins. Retros should not be only about problems.
